A letter for you

Dearest Ume Khair,

Happy Birthday. Of all the days in the year, this is the one I’m most grateful for, because it brought you into the world and, in time, into my heart.

You have a way of making ordinary moments feel like magic. Your smile quiets my worries, your laughter is my favourite sound, and your kindness reminds me what real beauty looks like.

I pray that Allah fills your life with happiness, peace and success, that He fulfils every wish you’ve ever whispered, and that you are always surrounded by people who love you as much as you deserve.

Thank you for being you: gentle, genuine and unforgettable. Whatever this new year brings, I hope you always know how deeply you are cherished.

Smile today, my dear, as brightly as you make everyone else smile.

Forever yours ♥
